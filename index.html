Latest Release: "A Seasoned Professional"

**A Seasoned Professional: Wrath of Angry Angel** follows the tumultuous journey of James W. Turner, a decorated detective wrongfully accused of murdering his wife, Marie Hoskins Turner, a respected state researcher. As he grapples with his innocence, a shocking betrayal leads him to a life-or-death escape from prison while his partner, Pearl Sawyer, risks it all to uncover the truth. As secrets unravel and deadly forces close in, both James and Pearl must navigate a web of lies, deceit, and hidden agendas to protect their loved ones and expose the real killer. With time running out and their lives at stake, can they reclaim their futures, or will past mistakes come back to haunt them? In a gripping tale of justice and redemption, loyalties are tested, and the line between right and wrong blurs in a world where everyone has something to lose.

Excerpt from A Seasoned Professional

James stood still and silent listening, then all of a sudden he shouted.

“Get down!”

Pearl dropped to the floor with her daughter in her arms and James dived to the floor too as bullets riddled through the whole house. The men opened up with sharp, precise three-round bursts, their fire guided by the infrared dots visible through their monocular. The Spray of bullets tore through the line of front windows, spewing glass shards everywhere. The men were getting closer.

Bullets gouged the walls and tore through the door, sending splinters flying. One of the men came and kicked down the door. Some men were now reloading after their guns had emptied out. James and Pearl stood from the debris and decided to pick out one man at a go. But there were too many and well-armed and they attacked from every side of the house. As James and Pearl fought off the men who had penetrated through, one of the perpetrators came at them through the front door. He froze for a bit then his firearm went off.
