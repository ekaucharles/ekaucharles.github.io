    143 (I Love You)
    Theme: Love · #Romance #Affection

    If I were a river
    My heart would be its source
    And your heart would be its mouth
    Its water would be called love
    Ever wondered why mountains don’t meet
    They don’t have feelings
    Love can move mountains

    Without you
    I’m all out of love
    I’m lost without you
    And can’t live without you
    I feel a blank space left within me
    A blank space that creates darkness
    Darkness amidst the light

    Two wrongs don’t make it right
    Ride with me
    And I’ll ride with you
    Off to the far off lands
    Where no one knows us
    Except for the sun, the moon and the stars
    Because I’ve got you
    And you’ve got me
    Nothing else matters.

    A Friend in Need
    Theme: Friendship · #Loyalty #Support

    Why do you
    Keep me close still?
    When we both took
    Two different paths
    Yet those close
    Are fleeing away from me

    You write to me,
    You text me
    Even when I don’t reply,
    You come face to face with me
    Even went to the lengths
    Of looking for my number

    Talk about the happiness
    Talk about the sadness
    The joy and sorrow
    All needed is a friend
    Who is never going to leave
    Stay with me no matter what.
